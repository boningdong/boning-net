Use a watch-sized PCB to test product-level system design.

I started this project to further develop my PCB and system-design skills with a compact, multilayer PCB design.

::: featured-link
[View source on GitHub](https://github.com/boningdong/SimpleWatch)
:::

# Design Goals

I designed a product-level smartwatch from scratch. The watch was designed to integrate a color LCD screen, capacitive touch interface, and heart-rate tracking. Despite its simple name, the design and engineering process were not simple.

# Development

The project followed these development phases:

- Planning the functionality
- Designing the hardware systems
- Choosing components
- Designing, manufacturing, and assembling the PCBs
- Developing the firmware

# Current Status

As of the July 28, 2019 update, I had finished designing and assembling the prototype board for firmware development and design validation. The project remained unfinished. Zero-ohm resistors allowed individual subsystems to be isolated during validation.
