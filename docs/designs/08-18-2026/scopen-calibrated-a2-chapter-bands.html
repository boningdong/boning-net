Project thesis

Bring the essential oscilloscope loop off the bench.

Scopen conditions an analog signal, samples it on a compact embedded system, then sends the waveform wirelessly to a desktop application. The system favors portability, affordability, and a clear interaction over laboratory-scale feature depth.

Chapter 03 / Firmware

Protect the sampling path from timing noise.

A wide chapter structure gives diagrams room to breathe while keeping the explanatory text at a readable line length.

The STM32 high-resolution timer triggers ADC sampling directly. DMA then moves the result into external SRAM, avoiding unpredictable context-switch timing in the critical path.

Five FreeRTOS threads divide communication and general logic. Semaphores coordinate the shared SPI interface and the empty and occupied slots in each queue.
